## Veo 3.1 Creation Modes

- **Text to video** — describe a scene, subject, action, lighting style, atmosphere, and camera move in natural language.
- **Image to video** — animate a still image while using its subject, palette, composition, and visual details as the starting point.
- **First and last frame control** — upload opening and ending images to guide the transition, transformation, reveal, or narrative beat between them.
- **Reference to video** — use character, product, object, or style references to improve consistency and visual direction.
- **Native audio generation** — create supported dialogue, ambience, music, and sound effects together with the video.
- **Prompt-driven camera direction** — request dolly moves, pans, close-ups, product tilts, tracking shots, or cinematic pacing.
- **1080p short-form output** — generate polished clips suited to ads, social content, concepts, product visuals, and story development.
- **Private project workflow** — create, manage, and review generations in a browser-based workspace with credit costs shown by workflow.

## How It Works

1. **Choose a workflow** — start from text, one source image, first and last frames, or reference assets.
2. **Direct the shot** — describe the subject, movement, camera, lighting, atmosphere, dialogue, and sound you want.
3. **Generate and iterate** — create a clip, review the result, adjust the prompt or inputs, and build the next version.
